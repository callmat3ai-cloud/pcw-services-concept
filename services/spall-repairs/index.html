Spall Repairs // Repairing Damage, Restoring Confidence

Removal of compromised concrete, rebar repair and coating protection or replacement, and concrete patching.

Scope of Work & Technical Capabilities

PCW Contracting Services executes this classification under California State License #699611 with dedicated field supervision and scope accountability tailored to occupied properties.

  • Concrete Decks – Removal of compromised concrete, rebar repair and coating protection or replacement, and concrete patching.
  • Parking Structures – Address corrosion-related deterioration, restore structural performance, and improve safety.
  • Concrete Exteriors – Restoring the strength, stability, and integrity of deteriorated or damaged concrete structures to recapture the architectural appearance.
  • Pool Vaults – Perform specialized concrete and rebar repairs to address moisture-related deterioration.
  • Bridges – Deliver certified and specialized spall repair solutions for pedestrian and vehicular bridges.

Who We Serve

HOA boards, property managers, and commercial asset owners across Southern California.

Process & Field Communication Approach

Every project includes daily reports, weekly projections, photo documentation, site walks, and resident updates – with townhall support for HOA and large-scale apartment clients.
