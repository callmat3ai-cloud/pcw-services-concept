FRP Systems // Fiber-Reinforced Polymer Structural Strengthening

Advanced carbon and glass fiber-reinforced polymer systems for concrete beam strengthening and load reinforcement.

Scope of Work & Technical Capabilities

PCW Contracting Services executes this classification under our verified California State License (#699611) with dedicated field supervision, line-item scope accountability, and systematic coordination tailored to occupied properties.

  • Carbon fiber-reinforced polymer (CFRP) beam, column, and slab strengthening
  • Structural upgrade without bulky concrete or steel beam additions
  • Surface preparation, concrete substrate profiling, and epoxy matrix saturation
  • Fire-rated protective coatings over installed composite laminates
  • Coordination with client project architects and consultants

Who We Serve

Commercial building owners, facility managers, and HOA communities requiring structural enhancement.

Process & Field Communication Approach

FRP installation demands strict adherence to substrate moisture and pull-off tensile strength requirements. PCW prepares surfaces properly and applies high-strength resin matrices.

Every project phase is supported by structured homeowner advance notices, on-site project coordination, and project documentation and communication accessible to property managers and board officers.
